India Public Finance Lab

Every line of the budget, open to everyone.

The detailed expenditure volumes of Indian state budgets, hundreds of thousands of printed account lines, turned into something you can explore, chart and question. Pick a state on the map, or start from a card below.

Research preview. The current build covers Karnataka FY 2018-19 to 2026-27 and Tamil Nadu FY 2014-15 to 2026-27. Read the method and data notes before using the figures.
