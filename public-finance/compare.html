How is percentage view calculated?

Percentage view divides each head by its state's total for the same measure and fiscal year: actuals by total actuals, revised by total revised, and budget by total budget. It is a spending share, not the percentage of the original budget spent.

Amounts are each state's printed volumes summed from additive leaf rows, shown in ₹ crore. Method and data notes.
