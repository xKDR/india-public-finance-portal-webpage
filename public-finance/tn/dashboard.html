Composition dashboard

Where the money goes, one ring at a time. Each doughnut shows the children of the current head for the chosen year and measure. Click a slice to open it, use the breadcrumbs to climb back. Hover any slice for the exact numbers; every ring is also a table.

Summary

Slices are shares of the parent's positive amounts; negative lines (recoveries) are excluded from the ring and listed in the table. Amounts are ₹ crore, summed from additive leaf rows. Method and data notes.
