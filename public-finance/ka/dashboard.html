Karnataka · state finances

Composition dashboard

Expenditure composition for the selected fiscal year and measure. The initial view shows major heads. Select a slice to follow the account hierarchy: major → sub-major → minor → sub-head → detailed → object head. Breadcrumbs and the displayed-level label identify the current branch. Percentages show each head’s share of its selected parent, excluding negative amounts such as recoveries. The table includes all amounts.

Slices are shares of the parent's positive amounts; negative lines (recoveries) are excluded from the ring and listed in the table. Amounts are ₹ crore, summed from additive leaf rows. Method and data notes.
